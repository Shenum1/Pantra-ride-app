Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

Collected automatically

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The mobile operating system and device model you use
  • Your push notification token
  • Crash and error reports

Information you provide

  • Name, email address, phone number, date of birth and address
  • Profile photo
  • Messages and support requests you send through the Application

Drivers also provide

  • Driver's licence and other verification documents, and a selfie
  • Vehicle details
  • Bank account details, used to pay out earnings

Payments. When you make a payment, your credit or debit card details are collected directly by our third-party payment processor, Flutterwave, through its secure, hosted checkout. Our servers do not receive, store or process your full card numbers. We only retain transaction reference IDs and truncated card details provided by Flutterwave (such as the card type, last 4 digits and expiry date) to verify payments and manage your billing history. The Application also keeps a record of wallet balances, payments, refunds and fares.

Trip information. Pickup and drop-off locations, routes, fares and trip history.

The information the Service Provider requests will be retained and used as described in this privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

Location Information

The Application collects your device's precise location to provide location-based features, improve the Application, and support related services. Drivers' location is also collected in the background while they are online or on a trip, so that riders can see the driver's position and trips can be completed.

  • Geolocation Services: The Service Provider uses location data to provide location-based features, such as matching, navigation and fares.
  • Analytics and Improvements: Aggregated location data may help the Service Provider understand usage patterns and improve performance.
  • Third-Party Services: Location data may be shared with third-party services used to support Application functionality, subject to this privacy policy and applicable law.

You can turn location off in your device settings, but trip features will not work without it.

Sharing between riders and drivers

Once a trip is accepted, limited information is shared between the rider and the driver to support a safe and successful pickup.

  • What drivers see about riders: first name, pickup location, drop-off location and live location.
  • What riders see about drivers: name, vehicle make, model and colour, licence plate number and live GPS location.
  • Calls and messages: riders and drivers can message each other in the Application and can call each other from it to coordinate the trip. A call uses the other person's phone number.

Advertising

The Application shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. You can turn personalised ads off in the Application's Privacy settings and in your device settings. On iOS, the Application asks for your permission before tracking.
