Delete your Pantra account

Pantra app, operated by Pantra Dev

You can ask for your Pantra account and personal data to be deleted at any time. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Open the Pantra app and sign in.
  2. Go to Privacy and choose Delete My Account. Drivers can find it under their Privacy and Security settings.
  3. Optionally tap Download a copy of my data first.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in or no longer have the app, email pylifekelvin@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Pantra account". Send it from the email address linked to your account and tell us the phone number on the account. To protect your security, we will verify your identity before processing the request, which may require you to confirm it from the email address or phone number linked to your active account. We will acknowledge your request within 48 hours and fully process the deletion within 30 days, subject to any data we are legally required to retain for tax, fraud prevention, or regulatory purposes.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email, phone number, photo, saved places, family contacts and payment details are erased.
  • Your messages and the written part of your reviews are erased.
  • If you drive for Pantra, your licence, vehicle, documents and bank details are erased, and you can no longer take trips.
  • Any reward points you still have are lost.

What is kept

Trip, wallet, payment, refund and fare records are kept for accounting and legal reasons, but they no longer show who you are. You can sign up again later with the same email, as a new account.

When deletion is not possible yet

To protect your money, an account cannot be deleted while any of these apply:

  • You have a trip that is not finished.
  • Your wallet still has money in it.
  • A refund to you is still being processed.
  • For drivers: a payout to you is still being processed, you have earnings that have not been paid out, or you owe Pantra commission from cash trips.

Once these are settled, you can delete your account.
