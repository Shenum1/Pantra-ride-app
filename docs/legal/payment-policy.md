## 1. Currency

All Pantra prices and payments are in **Nigerian Naira (₦)**.

## 2. How you can pay for a ride

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | You pay the driver the fare shown in the app, in cash, at the end of the trip. |
| **Pantra Wallet** | The fare is taken from your wallet balance at the end of the trip. |

You choose the method when you book. You cannot pay for a ride directly by card — to pay by card, top up your wallet first.

## 3. The Pantra Wallet

**Topping up.** Go to **Wallet → Add Money**. You will be taken to a secure payment page run by **Flutterwave**, where you can pay by card, bank transfer, USSD or another method Flutterwave offers.

- Minimum top-up: **₦100**. Maximum per top-up: **₦1,000,000**. **CONFIRM — currently configured.**
- Your wallet is credited only after Flutterwave confirms the payment to us directly.
- If you close the app during payment, Pantra checks the payment again the next time you open the app.

**Using the wallet.** Your wallet can be used for ride fares, waiting and cancellation charges, and tips. Your balance cannot go below zero.

**No withdrawals.** You cannot withdraw your wallet balance as cash or transfer it to another person. See the [Cancellation & Refund Policy](cancellation-refund-policy.md) for refunds.

**Expiry.** Wallet balances do not expire. **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED — confirm.**

**LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED — the wallet holds customer money. Confirm the regulatory position (including Central Bank of Nigeria rules on stored value and e-money), where the money is held, and what happens to it if Pantra stops operating.**

## 4. Your card details

Pantra never sees or stores your full card number, expiry date or security code (CVV). These are entered on Flutterwave's payment page and handled by Flutterwave. Pantra receives only the payment reference, amount, status and method type.

## 5. When you are charged

- **Wallet rides:** the fare is taken from your wallet when the driver completes the trip. There is no hold on your balance when you book, so make sure your balance covers the fare.
- **Cash rides:** you pay the driver at the end of the trip. The driver confirms in the app that payment was received.

**If your wallet does not cover the fare:** **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED** (see the [Cancellation & Refund Policy](cancellation-refund-policy.md), section 3).

## 6. What you pay

The fare shown before you book is what you pay for the trip itself. It is worked out as described in the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md), section 9. On top of it, you may pay:

- **Waiting charge.** Your driver waits for you free of charge for **3 minutes** after arriving. After that, a waiting charge of **₦40 per minute** is added to your fare when the trip starts. **CONFIRM — currently configured.**
- **Cancellation fee**, if you cancel late — see the [Cancellation & Refund Policy](cancellation-refund-policy.md).
- **Tip**, if you choose.

## 7. Discounts and rewards

**Promo codes.** A promo code gives a percentage off the distance-and-time part of your fare, sometimes up to a maximum amount. Each code has an expiry date and can be used once per person. The fare can never go below the minimum fare for your ride type. Pantra may withdraw a code that is being misused.

**Shared rides.** A shared ride is **20%** cheaper than the same ride on its own, on the distance-and-time part of the fare. **CONFIRM — currently configured.**

**Reward points.**
- You can earn points by watching optional reward ads (currently **20 points** per ad, up to **5** ads a day) and by completing reward tasks.
- **1 point is worth ₦16** off a ride.
- Points expire **90 days** after you earn them.
- You can use points at checkout to reduce what you pay. *(Pending implementation — the discount is not yet applied to the charged fare; see DEVLOG 2026-10-06, item 2)*
- Points have no cash value and cannot be transferred or withdrawn.

**CONFIRM — point values and limits are currently configured; Pantra can change them.**

## 8. Tips

- You can tip your driver after a completed and paid ride, from your **Pantra Wallet** only.
- Tips must be between **₦50** and **₦50,000** (whole Naira), and can be given within **72 hours** of the trip. **CONFIRM — currently configured.**
- **100% of the tip goes to the driver.** Pantra takes no commission on tips.
- Tips are optional and cannot be refunded once sent, unless made in error. **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED — confirm.**

## 9. Receipts

- **Wallet transactions:** you can view every top-up, payment, tip and refund in **Wallet**, and share or download a receipt.
- **Rides:** you can view a receipt for each ride in your trip history and receive it by email. *(Pending implementation — see DEVLOG 2026-10-06, item 10)*

## 10. Failed payments, duplicates, refunds and disputes

See the [Cancellation & Refund Policy](cancellation-refund-policy.md).

## 11. Drivers paying commission

Drivers who collect cash fares owe Pantra its commission. They can pay it in the app through Flutterwave, or by bank transfer or cash recorded by Pantra staff. See the [Driver Earnings & Payout Policy](driver-earnings-payout-policy.md).

## 12. Fraud

Do not use stolen cards, someone else's account, or fake payment confirmations. Do not tell a driver you paid when you did not. We may reverse credits, close accounts and report suspected fraud to the authorities. See the [Community & Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use-policy.md).
