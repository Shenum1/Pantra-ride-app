## 1. About Pantra

Pantra is a mobile app that connects people who need a ride ("riders") with drivers who offer rides in their own vehicles ("drivers"). Through the app, riders can request a ride, see the fare before booking, track the driver, pay, and rate the trip. Drivers can go online, receive ride requests, accept them, complete trips and receive their earnings.

## 6. How booking a ride works

1. You set a pickup point and a destination. You can use your current location, search for an address, or use a saved place (for example Home or Work).
2. You choose a ride type (Standard, Comfort or XL) and options such as priority pickup or a shared ride, if offered.
3. The app shows you the fare before you confirm (see section 9).
4. You choose how to pay: cash or Pantra Wallet.
5. When you confirm, your request is sent to drivers who are online. The first available driver to accept gets the ride.
6. You can see the driver's name, photo, vehicle details, plate number and rating, and follow the driver on the map as they come to you.
7. The driver marks when they have arrived and when the trip has started, and completes the trip at your destination.
8. Payment is taken at the end of the trip (see section 10). Both you and the driver can then rate each other.

**Scheduled rides.** You can choose a future pickup time. You will get a reminder before the pickup time. Your request may be visible to drivers before the scheduled time. **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED — confirm how scheduled rides should be offered to drivers.**

**Booking for someone else.** You can book a ride for another person and give their name and phone number. You must have their permission to share their details with the driver, and you remain responsible for the booking and payment.

**Shared rides.** If you choose a shared ride, you get a discounted fare. You may be matched with other riders going the same way. *(Pending implementation — matching is not live; see DEVLOG 2026-10-06)*

## 9. Fares

**You see the fare before you book.** When you request a ride, the app shows you the fare for each ride type. That fare is calculated by Pantra from:

- a base fare;
- a charge for the estimated distance;
- a charge for the estimated travel time;
- a minimum fare for each ride type (you never pay less than this for a completed ride);
- a booking fee;
- where they apply: higher demand pricing ("surge") when there are many requests and few drivers, a rush-hour adjustment at busy times, an airport pickup or drop-off fee, and a priority pickup fee if you choose priority.

Rates differ by ride type and may change from time to time. The rates that apply are the ones used to calculate the fare shown to you when you confirm.

**The fare does not change because of the route or traffic.** The fare is fixed when you book, based on the estimated distance and time. It is not recalculated at the end of the trip if the actual route or time is different.

**Charges that can be added after booking:**
- a **waiting charge** if the driver waits for you at pickup beyond the free waiting time (see the [Payment Policy](payment-policy.md));
- a **cancellation fee** if you cancel late (see the [Cancellation & Refund Policy](cancellation-refund-policy.md));
- a **tip**, only if you choose to give one.

**Discounts.** A shared ride, a promo code, or Pantra reward points may reduce your fare. Discounts apply to the distance-and-time part of the fare and never take it below the minimum fare. See the [Payment Policy](payment-policy.md).

All prices are in Nigerian Naira (₦).

## 10. Payments

You can pay for a ride with:

If you pay by wallet, the fare is taken from your wallet balance when the trip ends. Make sure your balance covers the fare before you book.

Full details — including failed payments, receipts, wallet rules, tips and promotions — are in the [Payment Policy](payment-policy.md).

## 11. Driver earnings and Pantra's commission

For every completed ride, Pantra keeps a commission. The commission is currently **10%**, charged on the distance-and-time part of the fare (the fare before booking, airport, waiting and priority fees are added). The driver keeps the rest. Pantra may change the commission rate; the rate that applies is the one in force when the ride is completed.

**BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED — confirm who keeps the booking, airport and priority fees. Today the driver keeps them (see DEVLOG 2026-10-06, item 18).**

On cash rides, the driver collects the whole fare and owes Pantra the commission. Full details are in the [Driver Earnings & Payout Policy](driver-earnings-payout-policy.md).
