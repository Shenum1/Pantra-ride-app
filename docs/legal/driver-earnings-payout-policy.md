## 1. What you earn from a ride

For each completed ride:

**Your earnings = the full fare paid by the rider − Pantra's commission.**

**Pantra's commission** is currently **10%** of the **distance-and-time part of the fare** — that is, the fare before the booking fee, airport fee, waiting charge and priority fee are added.

**Example** (for illustration only): the distance-and-time part of a fare is ₦3,000 and a ₦100 booking fee is added, so the rider pays ₦3,100. Commission is 10% of ₦3,000 = ₦300. Your earnings are ₦3,100 − ₦300 = ₦2,800.

**BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED — who keeps the booking, airport and priority fees. The example above reflects how Pantra currently works (the driver keeps them). If Pantra decides to keep them, this section and the example must change (see DEVLOG 2026-10-06, item 18).**

**Changes to the commission rate.** Pantra may change the commission rate. Each ride uses the rate in force when that ride is completed, so a change never affects rides you have already completed. **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED — how much notice drivers get before a change.**

**Waiting charges** go to you on the same basis as the fare.

**Tips** — you keep **100%**. Pantra takes no commission on tips.

**Cancellation fees** — when a rider pays a cancellation fee, you receive it less Pantra's commission. *(Pending implementation — see DEVLOG 2026-10-06, item 1)*

You can see your earnings for each trip and in total in the driver app.

## 2. Wallet rides and cash rides

**Wallet rides.** The rider pays Pantra from their wallet. Your earnings are added to your Pantra balance once the ride is completed and paid.

**Cash rides.** The rider pays you the full fare in cash. Because you already have the money, nothing is added to your balance — instead, **you owe Pantra the commission** for that ride. This is recorded as an amount you owe.

**Paying what you owe.** What you owe is settled by:
- being taken automatically from your earnings on wallet rides;
- paying in the app through Flutterwave; or
- paying by bank transfer or cash to Pantra, which Pantra staff then record against your account.

**Limit on unpaid commission.** If the commission you owe goes over **₦5,000**, you cannot accept new cash rides until you pay it down. You can still accept wallet rides. **CONFIRM — currently configured; Pantra can change it.**

## 3. Your available balance

Your available balance is:

> earnings from completed, paid rides + tips − commission you owe − payouts already requested or paid

You can see it in the driver app.

## 4. Getting paid (payouts)

**Bank account.** Add a Nigerian bank account in the driver app: bank name, account name and 10-digit account number. The account should be in your own name. **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED — confirm whether third-party accounts are allowed.** Your account number is stored encrypted; the app shows only the last 4 digits.

**Requesting a payout.** You request a payout from the driver app. The minimum payout is **₦100**. **CONFIRM — currently configured.** Pantra does not charge a fee for payouts. **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED — confirm, given Flutterwave transfer fees.**

**When you are paid.** **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED — payout timing.** At the moment, payout requests are checked and paid by the Pantra team rather than instantly. Do not promise a schedule until Pantra decides one (for example "within X working days of your request").

**Bank and provider delays.** Once a payout is sent, the time it takes to reach your account depends on Flutterwave and your bank.

## 5. Failed payouts

A payout can fail if, for example, the account number is wrong, the account name does not match, or the bank or payment provider rejects the transfer. If a payout fails:
- the Pantra team reviews it and may retry it, pay it another way, or mark it as failed;
- if it is marked as failed, the amount goes back into your available balance;
- you may be asked to check or update your bank details.

## 6. Adjustments

Pantra may adjust your earnings or balance when there is a legitimate reason, such as:
- a rider is refunded for a ride because of a fare error, a wrong charge or a trip that did not happen properly;
- a ride was recorded wrongly (for example, completed without the rider being picked up);
- fraud or misuse of the platform;
- a payment was reversed by the rider's bank or the payment provider.

We will tell you about an adjustment and the reason. **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED — whether refunds are deducted from the driver's earnings and how (today Pantra records the driver's share of a refund but does not deduct it).** **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.**
